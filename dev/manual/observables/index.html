Observables

Choose the quantities you want to measure, then give a writer the sampling schedule. You can measure the whole system or a named particle group.

Temperature and energy

all = Group(:all, AllSelection())
thermo = ThermodynamicObservable(all; name=:thermo)
writer = TableWriter("thermo.csv";
    every=100,
    observables=[thermo => [:temperature, :kinetic_energy,
                           :potential_energy, :total_energy]],
    mode=:replace,
)

Pass observables=[thermo] and writers=[writer] to your Simulation. Use total energy to assess an NVE run; for thermostatted dynamics, energy exchange with the bath is part of the model.

Pick a measurement

ObservableTypical fieldsUse it for
ThermodynamicObservable(group):temperature, :kinetic_energy, :potential_energy, :total_energy, :virialEquilibration and energy checks
VirialObservable(group):virial, :virial_accumulatedMechanical diagnostics
MSDObservable(group):msd, :elapsed_timeDisplacement and diffusion
VACFObservable(group):vacf, :elapsed_timeVelocity correlations
CollisionObservable():counts, :rate, :pair_counts, :pair_ratesEncounters between particle types
BathExchangeObservable():heat, :entropy, :entropy_production_rateExchange with stochastic baths

Available bath diagnostics depend on the chosen dynamics. For collision measurements, follow the interaction and cutoff setup in the two-temperature example.

Start a production interval

run!(sim, Stage(:equilibrate, steps=1_000))
reset_observables!(sim)
run!(sim, Stage(:production, steps=10_000))

reset_observables! clears accumulated measurements and sets a new displacement and correlation reference at the current configuration. It does not erase output already written to disk. Use writers to choose what to save and how often.

See the workflow reference for constructor arguments.
