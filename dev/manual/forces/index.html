Interactions

Describe interactions with force objects and pass them to Integrator(...; forces=...). All parameters use the same consistent units as your particle configuration.

Pair interactions

LennardJonesAttraction and short-range repulsionLennardJones(epsilon=1.0, sigma=1.0, cutoff=2.5)
WCAPurely repulsive Lennard-Jones interactionWCA(epsilon=1.0, sigma=1.0)

force = LennardJones(
    epsilon=1.0, sigma=1.0, cutoff=2.5,
    pairs=:neighborlist,
    neighborlist=CellList(buffer=0.5, capacity=256, rebuild_interval=10),
)

For small examples, pairs=:all avoids a neighbor-list setup. Its cost grows quadratically with particle count; use neighbor lists for larger systems.

Choose a neighbor list

CellList is a useful starting point for short-range fluids. Charged systems use the cluster-pair backend automatically. To choose it explicitly, pass neighborlist=ClusterPairList(buffer=0.1) on a force. Cluster pairs support 3D LJ and WCA systems; use the default cell-list route for 2D and soft repulsion.

The buffer extends the list beyond the interaction cutoff. Choose list capacity for the densest configuration you expect, especially under pressure control. Overflow stops the simulation instead of silently dropping neighbors.

ParticleDynamics.Workflow.ClusterPairList — Type
ClusterPairList(; buffer=0.1, prune_buffer=nothing)

Cluster-pair traversal for 3D Lennard-Jones (uniform or pair table) and real-space Coulomb: one fused kernel over a Newton-3 list of 8×8 particle tiles. The list radius is the largest interaction cutoff plus buffer; between rebuilds the list is pruned to the cutoff plus prune_buffer (default buffer/2). Bond exclusions of the vdW term are not supported (use CellList).

Lennard-Jones energy at the cutoff

Choose the energy convention explicitly when comparing models or engines:

  • vdw_modifier=:none (default) truncates the potential at the cutoff.
  • vdw_modifier=:potential_shift subtracts the value at the cutoff from each interacting pair's energy.
LennardJones(epsilon=1.0, sigma=1.0, cutoff=2.5,
             vdw_modifier=:potential_shift)

Forces inside the cutoff are the same for both conventions. Energies differ, which matters for energy comparisons and Monte Carlo volume moves. Use the same convention on both sides of a comparison.

Bonded models and mixtures

Use Topology to supply connectivity and combine pair forces with HarmonicBondForce, FENEBondForce, angle, dihedral or improper descriptors. The bonded guide covers parameters and labeling. For type-dependent pair parameters, use PairTable; its constructor is in the workflow reference.

FENE domain

FENE requires finite k ≥ 0, positive finite R0 with a representable positive finite square, and finite minimum-image bond lengths strictly below R0. Evaluation throws DomainError identifying an incident particle if a bond reaches or exceeds the limit. The potential and force are not clamped or continued beyond the physical domain. Near the limit they diverge; choose the timestep and initial configuration accordingly.

Physical helpers

These helpers convert between common particle properties and characteristic times. Keep their inputs consistent with the units of your model.

ParticleDynamics.Definitions.StokesFrictionCoefficient — Function
StokesFrictionCoefficient(η, R)

Return the Stokes drag γ = 6π η R for a spherical particle of radius R embedded in fluid with viscosity η. Handy when translating experimental values into the gamma input of Langevin or Brownian.

ParticleDynamics.Definitions.SphereMass — Function
SphereMass(ρ, R)

Mass of a solid sphere with density ρ and radius R: m = (4/3)π R³ ρ.

ParticleDynamics.Definitions.InertialTime — Function
InertialTime(m, γ)

Return the inertial relaxation time τᵢ = m / γ. Useful for picking dt relative to the physical time scales probed in the examples.

ParticleDynamics.Definitions.DiffusiveTime — Function
DiffusiveTime(R, γ, d)

Estimate the time required for a particle to diffuse its own diameter under isothermal conditions. Uses the relation τ_D = 4 R² γ / (2 d kT) with kT = 1 in the reduced units adopted throughout the repository.
