Bonded Terms, Minimization and NPT

All quantities are plain numbers in caller-chosen consistent units (length L, energy E, time). Angles are radians.

Typed bonded interactions

Topology sections carry optional labels; each force descriptor applies to the tuples whose label equals its type (:default when a section has no labels). Atom order is kept: the middle atom of an angle is the vertex, and torsions use the IUPAC signed dihedral φ = atan2(|b2| b1·(b2×b3), (b1×b2)·(b2×b3)) of the ordered quartet.

topology = Topology(bonds=bonds, bond_types=[:backbone, :side, …],
                    angles=angles, dihedrals=dihedrals, impropers=impropers)
forces = Force[
    WCA(epsilon=1.0, sigma=1.0),
    HarmonicBondForce(k=300.0, r0=1.0, type=:backbone),
    FENEBondForce(k=30.0, R0=1.5, type=:side),
    HarmonicAngleForce(k=30.0, theta0=deg2rad(115)),
    PeriodicDihedralForce(k=[1.0, 0.4], n=[1, 3], phase=[0.0, 0.0]),
    HarmonicImproperForce(k=10.0, chi0=0.0),
]
DescriptorPotential
HarmonicBondForce(k, r0)k(r-r0)²/2
FENEBondForce(k, R0)-kR0² log(1-r²/R0²)/2, strictly r < R0
HarmonicAngleForce(k, theta0)k(θ-θ0)²/2; θ0 = π is exact at collinear geometry
CosineSquaredAngleForce(k, theta0)k(cosθ-cosθ0)²/2
UreyBradleyAngleForce(k_theta, theta0, k_r, r13)harmonic angle + k_r(r13-r13⁰)²/2
PeriodicDihedralForce(k, n, phase)Σ k_m[1+cos(n_mφ-δ_m)]
OPLSDihedralForce(K1, K2, K3, K4)½[K1(1+cosφ)+K2(1-cos2φ)+K3(1+cos3φ)+K4(1-cos4φ)]
RyckaertBellemansDihedralForce(C)Σ Cn cos(ψ)^n, ψ = φ-π
HarmonicImproperForce(k, chi0)k·wrap(χ-χ0)²/2 (torsion-style, ordered quartet)
PeriodicImproperForce(k, n, phase)Fourier form on impropers

Every label must be assigned exactly once per section; missing or duplicate assignments are errors. Torsions and impropers require 3D. Invalid geometry (zero-length bonds, collinear torsion planes, FENE beyond R0) raises DomainError with the topology index. bonded_energy_components(st) returns per-family energies. Exclusion and 1–4 scaling policies beyond the existing bonded-pair exclusion are not implemented yet.

Energy minimization

result = minimize!(sim; method=FIRE(dt_initial=1e-3, dt_max=1e-2),
                   force_tolerance=1e-4, max_displacement=0.05,
                   mobile=Group(:solvent, TypeSelection(:W)))

minimize! works on a workflow Simulation (the integrator only supplies forces; no thermostat runs) or on a low-level SimulationState. The box is fixed; non-mobile particles keep their interactions but do not move. FIRE (velocity-Verlet FIRE) uses forces only; SteepestDescent uses a backtracking Armijo line search on energies and therefore stops with :step_too_small once energy differences fall below rounding (in practice about |F| ≈ √(2 k eps E_term)). result.converged is true only when the largest mobile force reached force_tolerance.

Isotropic NPT

barostat = MonteCarloBarostat(pressure=2.0, kT=1.0, interval=25,
                              log_volume_step=0.01, seed=1, tune=true)
# or StochasticCellRescaling(pressure=2.0, kT=1.0, compressibility=0.1,
#                            tau=0.5, interval=10, seed=1)
integrator = Integrator(dt=4e-3, scheme=BAOAB(), forces=forces,
                        methods=[Langevin(all; gamma=1.0, kT=1.0)], barostat=barostat)

Pressure is in E/L³, compressibility in L³/E. Both barostats scale all coordinates with the cell and leave velocities unchanged. They currently require 3D, scheme=BAOAB() with one Langevin method on all particles at the barostat kT, no freezing or external potentials. Long-range electrostatics follow the cell: the mesh influence function, reciprocal virial and background term are recomputed at every cell change. Monte Carlo moves use a log-volume proposal with the (N+1) Jacobian; stochastic cell rescaling follows Bernetti & Bussi (2020) with a fresh virial at every event. barostat_diagnostics(sim) reports volume, acceptance and committed changes. GSD frames record log/barostat/state; continue a run with restore_barostat!(sim, read_gsd_log(path, "log/barostat/state")).

States with rigid water (attach_rigid_water!, core API) take Monte Carlo volume moves only. The moves are molecular: set_cell_and_molecules! scales each water's centre of mass and translates the molecule rigidly, and the acceptance counts each water once. C-rescale and atom-wise scaling are rejected for rigid water.

Monte Carlo moves sample the potential energy itself, so the Lennard-Jones cutoff convention matters (vdw_modifier, see Forces). Use :potential_shift to compare with GROMACS.

Size the neighbor-list capacity for the densest volume the run will visit: an overflow stops the run instead of truncating the list. Each barostat event currently triggers a neighbor rebuild, so events every 10–25 steps are a reasonable default.
